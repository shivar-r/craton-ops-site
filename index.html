Craton Ops

Craton Ops is Our Babylon LLC's internal operations system. It connects Babylon's manufacturing and inventory system (Odoo) to Babylon's own QuickBooks Online company so that purchasing, production, shipping and invoicing are recorded in the books automatically.

It is used only by Our Babylon LLC staff and its accountant, for Babylon's own company. It is not offered to other businesses.
