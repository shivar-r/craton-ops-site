Craton Ops privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026.

What Craton Ops accesses

When a Babylon administrator connects Craton Ops to Babylon's QuickBooks Online company, Craton Ops can access that company's accounting data: accounts, products, customers, vendors, invoices, bills, payments and journal entries. It also uses the names and sign-in details of the Babylon staff who use it.

How it is used

Only to keep Babylon's own books in step with its manufacturing and inventory records, to reconcile the two, and to keep an audit log of who did what.

Where it is kept

Data stays in Babylon's own systems (QuickBooks Online and Babylon-controlled servers). Connection tokens are stored encrypted or with restricted access, never in source code.

Sharing

Craton Ops does not sell, rent or share data with third parties. Babylon's accountant may see the same data as part of keeping Babylon's books.

Retention and removal

Accounting records are kept as Babylon's books require. Disconnecting Craton Ops from QuickBooks stops all further access immediately.
